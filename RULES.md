# Rules of Play

## 1. Your Purpose

You govern an empire at war. Win by completing the chosen Scenario objective
before its final round while keeping **Rome**, **Senate**, and **Resolve** above
zero. You lose immediately if:

- Rome, Senate, or Resolve reaches 0;
- an enemy Army enters Aquileia;
- you cannot pay a required Legion loss; or
- a Scenario states another defeat condition.

Your score is:

`Objective VP + Rome + Senate + Resolve + Treasury - Fatigue - remaining Strength - Devastation`

## 2. The Map

The map has nine connected spaces. Five are Roman bases:

`Aquileia - Virunum - Lauriacum - Carnuntum - Sirmium`

Four are enemy settlements or campaigning regions:

- **Raetian Frontier**, connected to Lauriacum.
- **Marcomannia**, connected to Lauriacum and Carnuntum.
- **Lands of the Quadi**, connected to Carnuntum and Sirmium.
- **Plain of the Iazyges**, connected to Sirmium.

Each enemy people has:

- one **Army marker**, physically placed on the map; and
- one **Strength cube**, tracked from 0-6 in its homeland.

The four Armies are Raetian, Marcomanni, Quadi, and Iazyges. An Army may move from
its homeland into Roman bases. Its Strength travels with it; only the Army
marker moves. This is the entire opposition army system.

Each Army has one doctrine. It applies whenever its condition occurs:

- **Raetian - Alpine Pressure:** after it Musters to Strength 4 or more, lose
  1 Supply.
- **Marcomanni - Deep Incursion:** when it devastates a Roman base, lose 1
  additional Rome.
- **Quadi - Fractured Kingship:** whenever it Musters, increase Coalition by 1.
- **Iazyges - Sarmatian Horsemen:** in a Clash, the Enemy gains +1 unless Rome
  commits at least two Legions.

The **Coalition** track runs from 0-5. It measures cooperation, shared
opportunity, and pressure among Rome's enemies. When it fills, one additional
Army acts, then the track returns to 0.

Any space may hold any number of Legion cubes and Armies. Printed lines are the
only legal movement connections. Rivers and roads are already represented by
those lines; there are no movement points, terrain costs, facing, or supply
lines to count.

Tracks run from 0-7 unless noted:

- **Rome:** public security and confidence.
- **Senate:** cooperation, legitimacy, and elite support.
- **Resolve:** Marcus's capacity to continue bearing office.
- **Treasury:** money, transport, gifts, and administrative capacity.
- **Supply:** food, remounts, equipment, and river logistics.
- **Fatigue:** army exhaustion, 0-6.
- **Mercy:** restraint and durable settlement, 0-6.

## 3. Setup

1. Place the mat and choose a Scenario.
2. Set tracks, Strength, Armies, Legions, round limit, and special rules from the Scenario
   card. Location abbreviations are printed in the map legend below.
3. Shuffle the 16 Command cards. Draw three.
4. Build the Crisis deck from the Scenario's listed Crisis groups. Shuffle each
   group separately and stack later groups beneath earlier groups.
5. Place the remaining Crisis cards aside.
6. Set Coalition to 0. Put two dice nearby. Begin Round 1.

For a first game use **S01 The Gathering Storm**.

Scenario abbreviations: Aq Aquileia, Vi Virunum, La Lauriacum, Ca Carnuntum,
Si Sirmium, Ra Raetian Frontier, Ma Marcomannia, Qu Quadi, Ia Iazyges.

## 4. Round Sequence

### I. Receive the Crisis

Reveal the top Crisis. Apply its **Arrival** text from left to right. If it has a
choice, choose before changing any other state.

### II. Deliberate

You may discard one Command card to draw one replacement. The discarded card
is lost until the Command deck reshuffles.

Then choose one card from your hand and use exactly one half:

- **IMPERIUM:** its military order; or
- **OFFICIUM:** its civil or political order.

After resolving it, discard it. Draw back to three cards.

### III. Issue Two Basic Orders

Take two different Basic Orders. A card effect may permit repetition.

- **March:** move up to two Legions across one printed connection each. They may
  move together or separately. Resolve one move completely before the other.
- **Fortify:** pay 1 Supply to reduce an Army's Strength by 1 if it shares a
  space with a Legion or is adjacent to a Roman base containing a Legion.
  Alternatively, restore one devastated Roman base in or adjacent to a space
  containing a Legion; remove its Devastated marker and gain 1 Rome.
- **Campaign:** fight one battle where an Army shares a space with a Legion.
- **Petition:** pay 1 Treasury to gain 1 Senate, or send envoys to reduce
  Coalition by 2. If peace is available, you may Negotiate instead.
- **Requisition:** gain 2 Supply; lose 1 Rome or 1 Senate.
- **Meditate:** gain 1 Resolve; if no battle is fought this round, also reduce
  Fatigue by 1 at round end.

### IV. Enemy Design

Resolve the Crisis card's **Design** text, then its single **Army Order**.
Afterward increase Coalition by 1.

- **MUSTER:** increase that Army's Strength by 1. If already at 6, RAID instead.
- **RAID:** if the Army already shares a space with Legions, resolve a Clash
  without moving. Otherwise move it one connection along the shortest route to
  Aquileia. If two routes are equally short, use the red invasion arrow.

If a card says **Highest Army**, activate the Army with the highest Strength.
Break ties in favor of the Army closest to Aquileia, then choose.

After a RAID:

- If the Army enters a space with Legions, resolve one **Clash**.
- If it enters an empty Roman base, lose 1 Rome and either 1 Supply or
  1 Treasury. Leave the Army there.
- If it enters an empty Aquileia, you lose immediately. If Legions are there,
  resolve a Clash; you lose if the Army remains in Aquileia afterward.

No other Army moves, rolls, checks a chart, or takes an action.

If Coalition reaches 5, return it to 0 and resolve a **Coalition Surge**:

1. Exclude the Army named by the Crisis.
2. Choose the remaining Army with the highest Strength. Break ties in favor of
   the Army closest to Aquileia.
3. If it is in its homeland below Strength 3, it Musters. Otherwise it Raids.

A surge therefore requires no card draw or behavior chart and happens at most
once per round.

When an Army Raids an empty Roman base, place a **Devastated** marker there
after paying the normal losses. A devastated base remains connected and may
hold Legions, but grants no Base Support. Each unrestored Devastated marker is
worth -1 point at the end of the Scenario.

### V. Endure

1. If you fought, increase Fatigue by 1. If you fought twice, increase it by 2.
2. If Fatigue is 5+, lose 1 Resolve.
3. Check the Scenario objective and other defeat conditions.
4. Advance the round. If the final round ended, score the Scenario.

## 5. Battle

1. Choose a space containing an Army and at least one Legion.
2. Commit any number of Legions in that space. Pay 1 Supply per committed Legion beyond
   the first.
3. Choose one battle doctrine:
   - **Contain:** add +1 Roman total. The battle grants no Momentum, and even a
     win by 3+ retreats the Army only one connection.
   - **Set Battle:** use the standard rules and retain the option to Exert.
   - **Force Decision:** requires at least two committed Legions. Add +2 Roman
     total and increase Fatigue by 1. On a tie or loss, remove one additional
     committed Legion after resolving the normal result.
4. Roll one Roman die and one Enemy die.
5. Roman total = committed Legions + Roman die + bonuses.
6. Enemy total = that Army's Strength + Enemy die + Scenario bonuses.

Add **+1 Roman total** if the battle is in a Roman base, or if a connected Roman
base contains at least one Legion. This is **Base Support**. Several bases never
grant more than +1.

Resolve the difference:

- **Rome wins by 3+:** reduce Strength by 2, retreat the Army to its homeland,
  and gain 1 Momentum.
- **Rome wins by 1-2:** reduce Strength by 1, retreat the Army one connection
  toward its homeland, and gain 1 Momentum.
- **Tie:** reduce Strength by 1; the Army does not retreat. Lose 1 Supply or
  increase Fatigue by 1.
- **Rome loses by 1-2:** lose 1 Supply and 1 Resolve.
- **Rome loses by 3+:** remove one committed Legion and lose 1 Rome. If the
  battle is in a Roman base, the Army remains there.

Momentum is not a track. Mark each point on the Scenario card. It never exceeds
the Scenario objective. Some objectives require Momentum against a named Army;
record those points beside that people.

When using Set Battle, you may take one **Exertion** before rolling: gain +2
Roman total, then increase Fatigue by 1 after battle.

### Clash

A Clash is an enemy-initiated battle and takes only one roll:

1. Commit any number of Legions already in the Army's new space. Pay no Supply.
2. Roman total = committed Legions + Roman die + 1 if in a Roman base.
3. Enemy total = Army Strength + Enemy die.
4. On a Roman win, reduce Strength by 1 and retreat the Army one connection
   toward home.
5. On a tie, both sides remain.
6. On a Roman loss by 1-2, lose 1 Resolve.
7. On a Roman loss by 3+, remove one committed Legion and lose 1 Rome.

A Clash grants no Momentum and causes no Fatigue. You may use Assent, but not
Exertion or Command-card battle effects.

## 6. Stoic Choice

Once per round, after any die roll, you may accept the result without modifying
or rerolling it. Gain 1 Resolve or 1 Mercy. This is called **Assent**.

You cannot gain Assent when the unmodified roll already guarantees a win by 3+.
The rule rewards accepting danger, not harvesting certainty.

## 7. Peace

If an Army is in its homeland at Strength 1 or less and you have at least one
Legion there, you
may use Petition as **Negotiate**:

- spend 1 Senate or 1 Treasury;
- reduce that Strength to 0;
- gain 1 Mercy; and
- turn or remove the Army marker to show a treaty;
- leave one Legion there as its garrison. That Legion may March normally later,
  but doing so immediately breaks the treaty: return the Army at Strength 1.

Strength 0 with a garrison is a pacified people. If a Crisis activates a
pacified Army, return it to its homeland at Strength 1 instead of resolving the
printed order. Only one marker moves.

## 8. Command Deck

When you must draw and the Command deck is empty, shuffle its discard pile.
Then increase Fatigue by 1. The administrative machine is being used again
without rest.

## 9. Scenario Results

- **Decisive:** win and score 18+.
- **Hard Peace:** win and score 12-17.
- **Pyrrhic Duty:** win and score 11 or less.
- **Defeat:** fail the objective or trigger a loss condition.

For the Grand Campaign, carry forward tracks and surviving Legions. Between
chapters set Supply to at least 2, reduce Fatigue by 2, and draw a fresh hand.

After scoring, read the Scenario's historical epilogue. Then record Marcus's
legacy from the pattern of your decisions:

- **The Lawgiver:** Mercy 3+ and at least one pacified people.
- **The Soldier Emperor:** Imperium exceeds Officium by 2+, or Force Decision
  was chosen at least three times.
- **Guardian of the State:** Officium exceeds Imperium by 2+.
- **The Philosopher in Command:** none of the above.

Legacy is descriptive, not bonus VP. It makes the manner of victory part of the
campaign record.

## 10. Clarifications

- A track cannot exceed its printed maximum or fall below zero.
- "Lose" is mandatory. "Spend" requires the resource; if you cannot spend it,
  you cannot choose that option.
- Strength reductions cannot pass below zero.
- An Army always retreats by the shortest route to its own homeland.
- An Army sharing a space with Legions does not cause another battle until Rome
  Campaigns or that Army receives a RAID order.
- Card text overrides this rulebook.
